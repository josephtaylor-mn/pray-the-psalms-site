Privacy Policy — Pray the Psalms

Effective Date: September 2, 2026 (revised)

---

Overview

Pray the Psalms ("the App") is a free prayer application for iOS, Android, and the web. This policy describes what data the App collects, how it operates, and your rights as a user.

---

1. What We Collect

Pray the Psalms can be used without an account. If you choose to create an account, we collect only the information needed to authenticate you and synchronize your prayer activity. Specifically:

  • Optional account information — your email address, name when supplied by your sign-in provider, and a generated account identifier.
  • Optional synchronized activity — favorite psalms or verses, aggregate prayer progress, and the last psalm and verse reached.
  • No advertising networks are present.
  • No in-app purchases are offered.
  • No crash reporting tools are embedded.
  • Anonymous analytics are collected as described in Section 3.

---

2. Local Storage and Optional Sync

The App stores your visual theme, translation choice, favorites, and prayer progress on your device. These features work without an account.

  • Is saved locally using your device's standard storage.
  • Favorites and prayer progress are sent to Supabase only if you choose to sign in.
  • Can be changed at any time within the App.
  • Is deleted when you uninstall the App.

When signed in, Supabase stores an account record and a copy of your favorites and prayer progress so they can be restored on another device. Each signed-in user can access only their own synchronized records.

---

3. Anonymous Analytics

To understand how people use the App (e.g., how many people open it each day, which psalms are most prayed, which translations are popular, what percentage of sessions reach the closing "Amen"), we use PostHog — a privacy-friendly product analytics service. PostHog stores its data on servers in the United States.

The App sends only anonymous, aggregate usage events such as:

  • Which psalm was opened, from which source (Today's Psalms, Browse, Random), in which translation.
  • Whether a verse was advanced and whether a psalm session reached "Amen."
  • Which translation or visual theme was selected.
  • The length of a search query and the number of results (the query text itself is never sent).
  • The screen being viewed.

Each device is assigned a random identifier, stored locally (on the web, in your browser's localStorage — no cookies are set). If you sign in, that analytics identifier is associated with your generated account identifier so activity can be understood across sessions. PostHog derives broad geographic information (country / region) from your IP address at the time of the request; the App does not store your IP address. Analytics events do not include your name, email address, precise location, verse text, search-query text, or contact data.

If you wish to opt out, you can block analytics traffic at the network level (e.g., a content blocker) or, on the web, disable JavaScript for this site.

4. Network Usage

The App is designed to work fully offline for prayer. Bible translations bundled with the App — the World English Bible (WEB) and King James Version (KJV) (both public domain), and the Literal Standard Version (LSV) (© Covenant Press, used under CC BY-SA 4.0) — are included in the download and require no network connection to read.

The in-app feedback form, when you voluntarily submit it, posts your written feedback to a server operated by the developer, which then files a GitHub issue. No personal information is attached to that request beyond what you choose to write.

Analytics (Section 3), voluntary feedback, optional authentication, and optional synchronization are the only routine network uses. No Bible text is fetched from the internet at runtime.

---

5. Third-Party Services

Pray the Psalms does not integrate with advertising networks or crash reporting services. Specifically absent:

  • Advertising networks (e.g., Google AdMob, Facebook Audience Network)
  • Crash reporting services (e.g., Crashlytics, Sentry)

The third-party services the App contacts are:

  • PostHog — anonymous product analytics. See Section 3.
  • Supabase — optional account authentication and encrypted cloud synchronization of favorites and prayer progress.
  • Apple and Google — optional sign-in providers; their handling of login information is governed by their respective privacy policies.
  • The developer's feedback endpoint — used only when you voluntarily submit the in-app feedback form.

---

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. An account is never required. A parent or guardian who believes a child provided account information may contact us to request deletion.

---

7. Data Security

All network traffic is transmitted over HTTPS. Supabase applies row-level security so authenticated users can access only their own synchronized records. Locally stored data remains subject to your device's security protections.

---

8. Your Rights

You may sign out at any time. To request access to, correction of, export of, or deletion of optional account and synchronized data, contact us at the address below. If you wish to remove the device identifier used for analytics, clear your browser's site data (web) or uninstall the App (mobile).

These rights are available regardless of location and include rights provided by laws such as GDPR and CCPA where applicable.

---

  • [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
