Privacy Policy — Pray the Psalms

Effective Date: April 13, 2026

---

Overview

Pray the Psalms ("the App") is a free prayer application for iOS and Android. This policy describes what data the App collects (none), how it operates, and your rights as a user.

---

1. No Data Collection

Pray the Psalms does not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal information. Specifically:

  • **No personal data** of any kind is collected.
  • **No user accounts** are created or required.
  • **No analytics or usage tracking** of any kind is performed.
  • **No crash reporting tools** or third-party SDKs are embedded in the App.
  • **No advertising networks** are present.
  • **No in-app purchases** are offered.
  • **No internet connection** is required or used at any time after the App is downloaded.

---

2. Local Storage Only

The App stores a single preference on your device: your chosen visual theme (Dawn, Forest, Ocean, Twilight, or Night). This preference:

  • Is saved locally using your device's standard storage.
  • Never leaves your device.
  • Is never transmitted to any server, third party, or service.
  • Can be changed at any time within the App.
  • Is deleted when you uninstall the App.

No other data is written to your device beyond the App's own files installed during download.

---

3. No Internet Communication

Pray the Psalms operates entirely offline. The App makes no network requests of any kind. No data is sent to or received from any external server, API, or service after the initial download from the App Store or Google Play.

---

4. No Third-Party Services

Pray the Psalms does not integrate with or share data with any third-party service, including but not limited to:

  • Analytics platforms (e.g., Firebase, Mixpanel, Amplitude)
  • Advertising networks (e.g., Google AdMob, Facebook Audience Network)
  • Crash reporting services (e.g., Crashlytics, Sentry)
  • Cloud storage or sync services
  • Social media platforms

---

5. Children's Privacy

Because Pray the Psalms collects no data from anyone, it is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13 (COPPA) and children under 16 (GDPR). No personal information from children — or anyone — is ever collected.

---

6. Data Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no personal data to secure externally. All locally stored preferences (your chosen theme) remain on your device and are subject to your device's own security protections (e.g., device passcode, encryption).

---

7. Your Rights

Because no personal data is collected, there is no personal data to access, correct, export, or delete. If you wish to remove all App data, simply uninstall the App from your device.

For users in the European Union (GDPR) and California (CCPA): we have no personal data on file, and therefore there is nothing to fulfill under data subject access or deletion requests. You are welcome to contact us to confirm this.

---

8. App Store and Device Platform Data

When you download Pray the Psalms from the Apple App Store or Google Play, those platforms may collect information about the download according to their own privacy policies. That data is collected and controlled by Apple or Google, not by us. Please review:

  • [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
  • [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

---

9. Changes to This Policy

If the App ever changes in a way that affects data practices, this policy will be updated, and a new effective date will be posted. Given the App's design philosophy of collecting no data, such changes are not anticipated.
