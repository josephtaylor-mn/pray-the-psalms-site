THE METHOD

Praying the Bible

by Donald S. Whitney

The method is simple: go through a psalm line by line, talking to God about whatever comes to mind as you read each verse.

Each day, you have 5 psalms to choose from based on the day of the month. Scan them quickly and pick the one that best matches where your heart is today.

The first psalm matches today's date. Each additional psalm adds 30:

  • Day 1 → Psalms 1, 31, 61, 91, 121
  • Day 15 → Psalms 15, 45, 75, 105, 135

Read each verse slowly. Pause. Pray about whatever the verse brings to mind. When nothing more comes, move to the next verse.

You'll never pray the same prayer twice.
